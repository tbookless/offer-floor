# Accessibility

Offer Floor should be usable by every driver, including people who use screen readers, larger text, keyboards or dark mode.

## What's supported

- Designed for current mobile and desktop browsers: Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on computers.
- Every input has a visible label that screen readers announce.
- The verdict ("Take it", "Marginal" or "Decline") is shown in words as well as color, and screen readers announce it when it changes.
- The page can be zoomed and follows your phone's text size and light or dark setting.
- Buttons, links and fields show a clear outline when reached with a keyboard.
- Everything can be used with a keyboard, including **Setup → Restore from file**. In the section tabs (Check, Pickups, Setup, About), the arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs.
- Text and labels meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1 in both light and dark mode.

## Known limitations

None known right now. If you find one, please tell me.

## Reporting a barrier

If something gets in your way, please [open an issue](https://github.com/tbookless/offer-floor/issues/new/choose). Say what you were trying to do, what got in the way, and which phone, browser and assistive technology you use.
