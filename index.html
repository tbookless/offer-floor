      How the numbers work

      Hours = round-trip miles ÷ your average speed + drive time on the job + handoff time.

      Floor = your floor rate × hours, or the paper break-even (miles × IRS rate), whichever is higher. Below it: decline.

      Worth it = the same, at your target rate.

      Gas = miles × gas price ÷ mpg. That's the cash the trip actually costs you, and it is usually far below the IRS rate.

      Paper break-even: if you deduct mileage at the standard rate, a job paying less than miles × the IRS rate lowers your taxable profit. That isn't a cash loss — it's a bookkeeping line. The real reason to decline a far, cheap job is usually the hours.

      Chained jobs: if your car is already parked at the pickup from an earlier job, tick the box — the new job costs you time only, no new miles.

      These are estimates to help you decide, not tax advice.

      Privacy

      This is a static page. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server that receives your data. Your pickups, addresses and rates are stored in this browser on this device only.

      The only time anything leaves your phone is when you tap Look up in Maps, which opens your maps app with the two addresses.

      Tip: on iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app, works offline, and Safari won't clear its data after a week of not visiting.
