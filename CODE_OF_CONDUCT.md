# Code of conduct

Offer Floor is a small, free tool made by a driver for other drivers. Everyone who takes part here — opening issues, suggesting ideas, commenting or sending changes — is expected to keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including with people who are new to GitHub.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback about the idea, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic: making Offer Floor better for drive-away drivers.
- Protect privacy — yours and other people's. Don't post home addresses, pickup addresses, pay stubs or anything else that identifies a driver.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or promoting other paid services.
- Complaints about specific companies, dispatchers or customers. This project isn't the place for them.

## What happens

The maintainer ([@tbookless](https://github.com/tbookless)) may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and block people who don't follow this code.

## Reporting

If something here is a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (the **…** menu), or GitHub's [report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). For anything that's fine to discuss in public, open an issue.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
